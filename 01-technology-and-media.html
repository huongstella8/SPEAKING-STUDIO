Reasoning & structure

The same question, answered three ways. What separates the bands is not length but the reasoning operations performed: stating and illustrating at Band 6, conceding and categorising at Band 7, redefining and weighing trade-offs at Band 8.

Recording studio

Choose a question set rather than a single question. The examiner reads the first question; answer it, then press Next question and keep going. The recorder runs continuously across the whole set, exactly as in the real interview.

    Submission report

    Download the formatted report together with your audio file, then upload both to the link your teacher has given you. The two files share a submission ID so they can be matched after upload.

    The report records your name, email, submission time, module, question set, number of takes, total practice time, the duration of your final take, your full transcript, and every self-assessment statement with its result. Open it and print to PDF if a PDF is required.
